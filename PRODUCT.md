# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People keeping a personal, map-based record of meaningful life moments. They return to Life Trace to add a moment, revisit a period of their life, and notice the shape of their story over time.

## Product Purpose

Life Trace turns individual memories, places, photos, and threads into a personal archive that remains easy to explore. Success means a user can orient themselves in their history and re-enter a specific moment or period without friction.

## Brand Personality

Personal, calm, alive. The interface should feel like a considered private archive, never a social feed or a generic productivity dashboard.

## Anti-references

Avoid noisy social-media feeds, gamified dashboards, sterile data visualizations, and decorative interfaces that obscure the memory itself.

## Design Principles

1. Make time feel tangible, not tabular.
2. Keep the archive quiet enough for the memories to carry emotion.
3. Reward exploration with clear, immediate paths into detail.
4. Use visual character with restraint, never at the cost of orientation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast expectations, keep every interactive path keyboard accessible, use labels in addition to color, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
